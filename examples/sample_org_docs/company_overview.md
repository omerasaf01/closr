# NovaFlow Analytics — Company Overview

## Who We Are
**NovaFlow Analytics** is a B2B SaaS revenue intelligence company founded in 2022 with headquarters in Boston, MA and a European operations hub in Amsterdam, Netherlands. We build AI-powered revenue operations (RevOps) and predictive churn intelligence software for mid-market SaaS and subscription e-commerce businesses.

## Mission & Value Proposition
Mid-market revenue teams lose 12–18% of annual recurring revenue (ARR) to preventable churn and fragmented funnel visibility across CRM, billing, and product telemetry tools. NovaFlow Analytics unifies those signals in under 48 hours—without a dedicated data engineering team—so RevOps, Finance, and Customer Success leaders can forecast net revenue retention (NRR) accurately and intervene 90 days before renewal risk turns into logo churn.

**Core Value Proposition:**
> Predictable net revenue retention in 48 hours: unify CRM, billing, and product usage data into explainable churn alerts and automated expansion playbooks.

## Key Differentiators
1. **Explainable 90-Day Churn Scoring:** Unlike black-box predictive models, every risk score cites exact behavioral drivers (e.g., seat utilization drop, unresolved P1 tickets, champion departure, invoice delays).
2. **48-Hour Zero-ETL Onboarding:** Native bidirectional connectors for Salesforce, HubSpot, Stripe, Chargebee, Snowflake, and Segment deploy without SQL pipelines.
3. **Enterprise-Grade EU & US Compliance:** SOC 2 Type II certified, ISO 27001 aligned, and full GDPR compliance with dedicated EU (Frankfurt) data residency for EMEA customers.
4. **Actionable Workflow Automation:** Pushes risk alerts, health summaries, and expansion triggers directly into Slack, Salesforce, Gainsight, and HubSpot sequences.

## Brand Voice & Communication Guidelines

- **Writing Style:** Lead with operational clarity and verifiable workflows; avoid generic "AI magic" claims or unsubstantiated ROI promises.
- **Visual & Copy Aesthetic:** Clean executive precision tailored to data-literate RevOps, CRO, and CS leaders.

- **EMEA (2026 Expansion Priority):** United Kingdom, DACH (Germany, Austria, Switzerland), Benelux, and the Nordics (currently 22% of ARR, targeting 35%).
