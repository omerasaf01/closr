# NovaFlow Analytics — FY2026 Marketing Brief

## Strategic Business Goals
1. **Pipeline Growth:** Grow qualified sales pipeline by 65% year-over-year, from $14M to $23M in qualified opportunities.
2. **EMEA Expansion:** Increase EMEA share of new bookings from 22% to 35% across the UK, DACH, and Nordic mid-market SaaS corridors.
3. **Capital-Efficient Acquisition:** Reduce blended CAC payback period from 14.5 months to under 11 months.
4. **Expansion & Cross-Sell:** Drive multi-module adoption (pairing *NovaFlow Pulse* with *NovaFlow Retain*) across 30% of existing Growth and Scale accounts.

## Explicit & Implied Marketing Needs
- **High-Intent Demand Capture & ABM:** Launch targeted LinkedIn ABM and high-intent search campaigns aimed at RevOps and Customer Success buyers evaluating churn prediction and NRR forecasting tools.
- **Technical Authority Content & Benchmarks:** Publish credible NRR benchmark reports, RevOps data-architecture playbooks, and comparison guides that win organic search and establish category authority.
- **EMEA Localization & Trust Positioning:** Highlight Frankfurt EU data residency, GDPR compliance, and regional case studies to overcome European procurement objections.
- **Ecosystem Co-Marketing:** Build joint webinars and integration launch campaigns with HubSpot, Stripe, and RevOps implementation consultancies.
- **Community-Led Growth:** Build authentic visibility in RevOps and SaaS leadership communities (e.g., RevOps Co-op, Pavilion, SaaStr circles, and targeted Reddit/LinkedIn discussions) without spammy outbound tactics.

## Existing Customer & ICP Hints
- **Company Size:** 50–500 employees, $10M–$120M ARR (Series B–D B2B SaaS and high-frequency subscription e-commerce).
- **Primary Buyers & Champions:**
  - VP / Director of Revenue Operations (RevOps)
  - Chief Revenue Officer (CRO) / VP of Sales
  - VP of Customer Success
  - Head of Finance / FP&A (influenced by NRR forecasting accuracy)
- **Common Buying Triggers:** Missed NRR targets for 2+ quarters, recent CRM/billing migration, upcoming board audit or Series C/D diligence, or loss of a dedicated data analyst.

## Budget, Team & Operational Constraints
- **Program Budget:** $180,000 per quarter ($720,000 annual non-headcount spend) covering paid media, content production, events, and tooling.
- **Team Capacity:** Lean 4-person marketing team (VP Marketing, Demand Gen Lead, Product Marketing Manager, Content Strategist); campaigns must prioritize high-leverage, repeatable systems.
- **Compliance & Brand Guardrails:**
  - Strict GDPR opt-in and CCPA compliance on all lead capture and retargeting workflows.
  - No unverified ROI percentages or fabricated customer quotes in ad copy.
  - Do not target SMB companies under 30 employees (high churn, poor unit economics).

## Target KPIs
- Marketing-sourced SQLs per quarter: 140+ (at least 45 from EMEA)
- Demo-to-SQL conversion rate: >= 42%
- Cost per SQL: <= $1,250
- Organic non-branded demo requests: +50% YoY
